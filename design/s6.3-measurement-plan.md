## What S6.3 measures (two payloads, both write back via the proven loop)

1. **Edge transfer deltas** — the cheaper-after-foundation saving for a prerequisite edge
   (findings 15,16). Method: train the successor cert from base (baseline GPU-steps-to-cert) vs from
   the foundation adapter (foundation→successor steps); `steps_saved_frac = (baseline − withFoundation)
   / baseline`, over `n_receipts` runs. A measured edge flips `unverified → confirmed` and earns a real
   shortcut on the tree. **Honest prior:** transfer is pair-specific and can be ≤0 (Pruksachatkun 2020) —
   a measured non-positive delta is a real, recorded result, not a failure.
2. **Technique recipe-preview fits** — per technique: a fitted data-mixing law (predicted loss/steps +
   calibration scale, Ye 2024 / CMR Gu 2024), the replay fraction + measured forgetting (Bethune 2025 /
   Kalajdzievski 2024), and the task fingerprints (Vu/Ilharco). Fills the `--preview` block.
   The mixing-law/forgetting fits come from a handful of SMALL calibration runs, not a full sweep.
